## Standing rules (apply to everything, not tied to one date)

- Paper only until James decides otherwise. Exception: sweep and (once turned on) Wheel2 CSP place real orders, but only against their own Alpaca **paper** accounts, never anywhere real.
- Claude suggests, James approves. No strategy or trading-logic change without his yes.
- 0DTE options only in the trading account. Long-term/premium-selling strategies are a separate research bucket (the wheel) — see STRATEGY_REGISTRY.md.
- 1 contract per paper trade; regular trading hours only.
- Try not to push code during market hours while positions are open — check for open positions first; this is a soft preference, not an absolute rule.
- Configuration is kept stable so samples can build before judging any one book — material changes (like Nov 13's) reset that book's sample clock, and James approved that tradeoff explicitly.
- Before going live: restore the daily trade/loss limits (currently off for paper). This is a standing reminder for every morning review.
- Robinhood trade approvals are off; no approval step wanted live.
- Two real-money accounts (Robinhood "BOT Trading", the Alpaca live brokerage account) and two wheel-research paper accounts (Wheel, Wheel2) are not to be touched for real/live trading until a live decision is made.
- New strategies run on the server as a journaled book on the dashboard, not as a separate standalone process James has to keep alive himself.
- **Security:** never echo pasted Alpaca/Anthropic API keys or secrets in plaintext anywhere in chat; never write them anywhere outside Render secret environment variables.
- **Deterministic authority:** order validation and risk enforcement stay in deterministic code. No AI model is ever the sole authority for a live order decision — see the AI overlay entry below for how that's enforced in practice.
- **Multiple video-sourced "levels" strategies, cross-session handoff pattern:** several strategies now arrive as a YouTube video James shares, watched and transcribed into a spec doc by a different Claude session (one with working video access), which the build session (which has code/repo access but not video access) then critically reviews and builds from. This is deliberate division of labor, not a workaround. Treat every such spec as an unverified best-effort transcript translation until its mechanical rules are checked, not as settled fact.
- **IB and BRT are tracked as separate strategies, not one "levels family," per James's explicit choice (2026-11-14)** — each one's variant count and results stand on their own in EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, even though both are level-based SPY/QQQ hypotheses from the same week.

## 2026-11-27 — Extended support/resistance levels on both decks (pushed, live)
James: "here's another idea for adding value to our support and resistance indicators. can we add fibinachi and also previous days high/low, previous days 4 hour, previous weeks high/how, previous months high/low. i'm sure you know more about these high value points. also, gex indicators where high volume is bunched up at. use your best judgement to enhance our support/resistance indicators."
**Display only. No entry, exit, sizing or risk rule reads any of these lines.** Whether price reacts at any of them is NOT tested; they are reference prices (same standing as the 2026-11-27 levels block). A later study may test them, using exactly the definitions below (also in the header of `levels_ext.js`).
- **Reference day:** today on a weekday, the coming Monday on a weekend. "Previous" is chosen by DATE (previous day = last daily bar before the reference day; previous week = last weekly bar starting before that Monday; previous month = last monthly bar starting before the 1st of the reference month). Daily/weekly/monthly bars are regular-hours bars from Alpaca (SIP feed when the plan allows, else IEX; the feed used is logged and in the payload). Verified 2026-11-27: Alpaca weekly bar for the week of 11/23 (H 729.15, L 695.45) equals the max/min of that week's daily bars.
- **Previous day / week / month high and low:** the bar's high and low. Major lines.
- **Fibonacci (prior-day retracement):** R = prior-day high minus low; up day (close >= open): high - r*R; down day: low + r*R; r = 0.382, 0.5, 0.618. Minor lines.
- **Previous-day 4-hour candles:** blocks 9:30-13:30 ET and 13:30-session close, high and low of each, from 30-minute bars inside the session; session close from the Alpaca calendar (11/27 closed 13:00, so only the first block exists and is marked partial). Minor lines.
- **Volume profile (previous session, 1-minute bars):** volume placed at typical price (H+L+C)/3 into bins of max(0.05, 0.03% of price rounded to 0.05); POC = busiest bin (major line); value area = smallest run of bins around POC holding 70% of volume, grown one bin at a time toward the busier side (ties up); VAH/VAL minor lines.
- **Open interest "where it is bunched" (a fact as published, as of the date shown, usually the prior trading day; today's new positions are not in it):** expirations within 7 days, strikes within 6% of price: OI call wall (most call OI at or above price), OI put wall (most put OI at or below), OI max pin (most total OI). Major lines.
- **GEX (an ESTIMATE):** dollar gamma per 1% move = gamma * OI * 100 * price^2 * 0.01, calls positive and puts negative (assumes customers are net long calls and short puts, so dealers hold the opposite; often wrong). Gamma is Black-Scholes using the implied vol Alpaca reports, rate 0, time to 16:00 ET expiry (min 30 minutes); it matched Alpaca's own reported gamma within 8% for SPY 726C. GEX call wall / put wall = strikes with the largest positive / negative net; gamma flip = the price in a +/-6% grid where total net GEX crosses zero, nearest the price. Minor lines, labelled "(est.)" on the page.

Lines closer together than half the near distance are merged into one label. Everything else is in an expandable "All levels" list sorted around the live price.
