## Standing rules (apply to everything, not tied to one date)

- Paper only until James decides otherwise. Exception: sweep and (once turned on) Wheel2 CSP place real orders, but only against their own Alpaca **paper** accounts, never anywhere real.
- Claude suggests, James approves. No strategy or trading-logic change without his yes.
- 0DTE options only in the trading account. Long-term/premium-selling strategies are a separate research bucket (the wheel) — see STRATEGY_REGISTRY.md.
- 1 contract per paper trade; regular trading hours only.
- Try not to push code during market hours while positions are open — check for open positions first; this is a soft preference, not an absolute rule.
- Configuration is kept stable so samples can build before judging any one book — material changes (like Nov 13's) reset that book's sample clock, and James approved that tradeoff explicitly.
- Before going live: restore the daily trade/loss limits (currently off for paper). This is a standing reminder for every morning review.
- Robinhood trade approvals are off; no approval step wanted live.
- Two real-money accounts (Robinhood "BOT Trading", the Alpaca live brokerage account) and two wheel-research paper accounts (Wheel, Wheel2) are not to be touched for real/live trading until a live decision is made.
- New strategies run on the server as a journaled book on the dashboard, not as a separate standalone process James has to keep alive himself.
- **Security:** never echo pasted Alpaca/Anthropic API keys or secrets in plaintext anywhere in chat; never write them anywhere outside Render secret environment variables.
- **Deterministic authority:** order validation and risk enforcement stay in deterministic code. No AI model is ever the sole authority for a live order decision — see the AI overlay entry below for how that's enforced in practice.
- **Multiple video-sourced "levels" strategies, cross-session handoff pattern:** several strategies now arrive as a YouTube video James shares, watched and transcribed into a spec doc by a different Claude session (one with working video access), which the build session (which has code/repo access but not video access) then critically reviews and builds from. This is deliberate division of labor, not a workaround. Treat every such spec as an unverified best-effort transcript translation until its mechanical rules are checked, not as settled fact.
- **IB and BRT are tracked as separate strategies, not one "levels family," per James's explicit choice (2026-11-14)** — each one's variant count and results stand on their own in EXPERIMENT_LOG.md, even though both are level-based SPY/QQQ hypotheses from the same week.
